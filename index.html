01 · What it does

One reading in, one next step out.

HUMN reads the health data you already have and turns it into a small, concrete action for today — then shows you honest progress, including on the days you miss.

01

Know what to do next.

Today shows one useful action drawn from your own readings — a walk, some daylight, winding down, or time for focused work. Choose it, and HUMN opens the setup. You decide whether to start.

02

A missed day isn’t a reset.

Missing readings are left out of your score rather than counted as zero. Your history is preserved, and a hard day is information plus a next step — not a reason to start over.

03

Your data, under your control.

Health values are read from Apple Health and computed on your device. Optional sync uses your own private iCloud. Export or delete your data whenever you want.

02 · What you need

Built for the phone in your pocket.

iPhone Required. HUMN runs on iPhone.
Apple Health Required for your signals. HUMN reads only what you grant, and computes your score on the device.
Apple Watch Optional. Phone-only works with fewer signals — the app adapts to what it can read.
03 · Privacy, plainly

Your biology stays yours.

No ads. No selling data. Here is exactly what HUMN does with your information.

On your device
Health values are read from Apple Health and computed on your iPhone. Your score, history and progress are derived locally.
Optional sync
If you turn on sync, your history, tasks and task photos are stored in your own private iCloud. HUMN does not run a server that holds your health values.
No ads, no selling
HUMN shows no advertising and does not sell your data. Crash diagnostics are scrubbed of health values. You can export or delete your data at any time.
