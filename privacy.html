HUMN Privacy Policy

Effective: April 25, 2026 Last updated: November 11, 2026

HUMN turns your Apple Health data into one useful next step and applies only the app restrictions you choose. The app is published by Chris Moore. Health values are read from Apple Health and scored on your iPhone.

1. Health values HUMN reads from Apple Health

HUMN reads the following Apple HealthKit types, and only after you grant explicit permission in iOS:

  • Time in Daylight
  • Step Count
  • Active Energy Burned
  • Apple Stand Hour
  • Heart Rate (for Max Output / high-intensity detection during workouts)
  • Heart Rate Variability (SDNN)
  • Resting Heart Rate
  • Respiratory Rate and Sleeping Wrist Temperature (read only, for the sick-day check; never scored)
  • Apple Exercise Time
  • Sleep Analysis (in-bed / asleep stages)
  • Blood Glucose (when a CGM is paired)
  • Workout Type (for strength-session detection and workout rollups)
  • Cardio Fitness / VO₂ max
  • Mindful Session / Mindfulness (for focus-timer scoring)
  • Dietary Protein (for opt-in protein scoring)
  • Date of Birth (for age-based Max Output thresholds)
  • Biological Sex (for alcohol heavy-day thresholds)
  • Number of Alcoholic Beverages (when you log drinks)
  • Dietary Caffeine (when you log caffeine)
  • Body Fat Percentage, Lean Body Mass, Height and Body Mass (weight), for the body composition readings

HUMN requests HealthKit write permission only for raw values you create in HUMN: alcohol drink counts, protein grams, caffeine (mg), body fat percentage, lean body mass, weight, Mindful Minutes from a focus session you finish, and daylight minutes from an in-app sun timer you stop. If you start a goal workout on Apple Watch, HUMN also saves that workout to Apple Health. HUMN writes these raw samples so Apple Health stays your source of truth. HUMN does not write scores, baselines, Shield state, app selections, or any other derived value back to Apple Health.

You can revoke any HealthKit permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → HUMN.

2. Where your data lives

  • On your device. Scores, histories, preferences, timer state, and cached

health-derived values are computed and stored on your iPhone. Your selections in Apple's Screen Time picker are represented by opaque tokens held by iOS; HUMN does not receive the specific apps, categories, or websites you chose.

  • Apple Health. Source measurements stay in Apple Health under your

permission settings.

  • Your own private iCloud (CloudKit). When you are signed in to iCloud on

your iPhone, HUMN saves your daily score history, your strength-workout claims, your tasks and task photos, and your Focus timer records to your private CloudKit database. This happens automatically and has no on/off switch in HUMN. If you are signed out of iCloud, these stay on your iPhone. This is your iCloud account, governed by Apple's service and account protections. HUMN has no server of its own that holds this data, HUMN's servers never receive your tasks, and the copy is used only to move and restore your own records on your own devices.

  • Apple Home. HUMN asks for Apple Home access only if you tap "Choose from

Apple Home" to pick a smart plug. It switches only the plugs you choose, at the TV and coffee cutoff times you set. Nothing about your home leaves your iPhone except through Apple's HomeKit framework.

  • Camera. HUMN uses the camera only when you scan a Nutrition Facts label

to read its protein grams. The scan runs on your iPhone. No photo is stored and no image leaves the device.

  • Crash diagnostics. Limited JavaScript crash-location reporting is

described in section 4.

  • Native crash and hang diagnostics. iOS gives HUMN its own crash, hang

and app-exit diagnostics through Apple's MetricKit. HUMN keeps the most recent ones on your iPhone only, never transmits them, and shares one only when you tap Share. They contain app and system details such as the app version, device model and code locations, not health values.

3. How your data is used

Health-derived information supports your scores, history, personal baselines, widgets, Focus timers, and the restrictions you choose. HUMN does not show ads, does not sell or rent your data, and does not send health values to third-party analytics. On-device diagnostic screens and exports can contain sensitive health information, so a screenshot or export you share discloses whatever it contains to the recipient you pick.

4. Services and diagnostics

Apple provides HealthKit, iCloud/CloudKit, Screen Time, and app distribution. Expo delivers app updates. These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Production builds can configure Sentry for JavaScript crash-location reporting. Events are rebuilt from an allowlist: event metadata, app release, and source file/line/column locations. Arbitrary exception text, messages, health values, user context, and breadcrumbs are removed before sending. Native crash reporting, performance transactions, session tracking, screenshots, and view-hierarchy capture are disabled. Crash reporting is off unless the developer configures a reporting endpoint.

5. Retention and reinstalling

The displayed daily-score history retains a limited recent window on your device; other histories and settings have their own storage lifetimes. Your private iCloud records can remain beyond that window. Uninstalling removes on-device app storage, but Keychain records and iCloud copies can remain, and reinstalling can restore saved scores from your iCloud. If iCloud is unavailable or full, local scoring continues without backup.

6. Your controls

  • Export. Profile → Account → Export data produces a bundle of your

on-device histories and settings, plus your HUMN iCloud records when sync is in use. The file can contain sensitive health information.

  • Delete. Profile → Account → Delete clears HUMN's on-device histories and

settings and removes the HUMN records stored in your private iCloud. It does not delete records that remain in Apple Health, and it is not a remote wipe of another device. If you use HUMN on more than one device, close the app on the others first so unsynced local copies aren't re-uploaded.

  • Revoke access. Manage HealthKit and other permissions in iOS Settings.

7. Children

HUMN is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not designed for children.

8. Security

HUMN uses iOS storage protections and SecureStore/Keychain for selected values; some histories use standard app storage. No system is perfectly secure. Report suspected vulnerabilities to moorecash@gmail.com.

9. Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be reflected in the updated date.
