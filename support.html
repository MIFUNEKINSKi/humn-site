Getting started

What does "FIRST WEEK · X OF 7 SCORING DAYS" mean?

During your first week HUMN is still learning your baseline, so score-based blocking stays off. The counter is scoring days, not calendar days: it advances only on days HUMN saved a reading. The seven days need not be consecutive. A missed day doesn't reset the counter, and today only counts once a reading has been written.

Protections you turn on yourself — Focus, Digital Sunset, Circulation Guard — apply immediately and are unaffected by calibration.

Why is a signal missing?

HUMN reads Apple Health. A missing or stale value is usually one of:

  • Apple Health permission was changed. Open iOS Settings → Privacy &

Security → Health → HUMN and re-grant any access that shows as off.

  • A sensor hasn't synced yet. Some watches (non-Apple watches, or

third-party CGM and sleep apps) write to Apple Health through their own app, often in delayed batches. HUMN can only read what has landed in Apple Health, so a value may appear minutes or hours after the measurement.

  • The hardware doesn't provide that signal. Phone-only works with fewer

signals; Apple Watch adds more.

Missing readings are left out of your score rather than counted as zero. The score re-weights around what is readable, so an absent signal is never treated as a bad result. Pull-to-refresh on the Dashboard forces a fresh Health sync.

How do I set up optional hardware?

Optional sensors and logging features live in Profile and can be set up at any time. Advanced or experimental hardware setup is behind Developer tools and is not required to use the phone core.

Protections and the Shield

Why is an app blocked?

HUMN only shields the apps and categories you selected yourself. Work, banking, communication, navigation, reading, 2FA, and accessibility tools should stay out of that selection.

A Shield can engage for several reasons, and each one names its actual reason:

  • Score rule: Standard mode shields only in Ash. Hard Mode shields your

selected apps whenever the score is below your line.

  • Digital Sunset: only if you enable it, selected apps shield during your

wind-down window.

  • Circulation Guard: after a long stretch of stillness, selected apps shield

until your next movement reading arrives.

  • Focus sprint: while a Focus session you started is running, selected apps

can stay shielded until it ends.

If a Shield looks wrong, open HUMN and pull-to-refresh. The app reconciles the current score, sunset window, sprint state, and Circulation Guard.

The Shield won't dismiss after I moved.

Score-based Shields clear when the score recovers. Circulation Guard clears after your movement reading arrives. iOS background delivery and delayed sensor feeds (for example, a non-Apple watch syncing through its own app) can lag, so there may be a delay between the movement and HUMN receiving the sample. Opening HUMN to the Dashboard forces an immediate Health sync.

Will the Shield block me on a recovery day or during calibration?

No. During calibration and on a recovery day, score-based blocking stays off. Missing readings never create a block. An explicit Focus or Digital Sunset protection you turned on still applies, and it always shows its real reason and an exit.

What is grace in Hard Mode?

Hard Mode is a stricter contract you opt into. It includes a rolling grace window: a small number of completed below-line days do not arm the score Shield, so one hard day doesn't lock you out. Missing dates and days with no reading do not consume grace. When grace is spent, the Shield states the real reason.

Does the evening / TV wind-down run automatically?

No. The evening wind-down and any TV or screen shutdown are off until you turn them on and configure them yourself. Nothing is selected or scheduled automatically. You can change or disable them at any time.

Hard Mode disabled itself overnight.

When you disable Hard Mode, the change takes effect 24 hours later — a deliberate cooling-off window so an impulsive moment doesn't override your earlier choice. Re-enabling is one tap, with no delay.

Data: export and delete

How do I export my data?

Profile → Account → Export data. HUMN produces a bundle of your on-device histories and settings, and — when you are signed in to iCloud — the records HUMN stores in your own private iCloud. Apple's Share sheet handles where it goes (save to Files, AirDrop, email, and so on). An export can contain sensitive health information, so share it only with people you trust.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Account → Delete. After confirmation, HUMN clears its on-device histories and settings, and removes the HUMN records it stored in your own private iCloud. This is permanent and cannot be undone. If you use HUMN on more than one device, close the app on the others first so unsynced local copies aren't uploaded again. Deleting HUMN data does not delete records that remain in Apple Health.

Privacy

HUMN's privacy policy lives at privacy.html.

Short version: health values are read from Apple Health and computed on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, your score history, tasks and task photos are saved in your own private iCloud. There are no ads, your data is never sold, and health values are never used for third-party analytics. See the privacy policy for details.
